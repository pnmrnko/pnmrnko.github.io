I’m going to read *Alien*, the novel Alan Dean Foster wrote based on the 1979 film. I’ve always liked the Alien films, especially the first one. Damn, it’s a science fiction classic!

It will be my first time reading a book based on a film script. It caught my interest because a Ukrainian translation came out, and most online reviews were positive. Although I used to be sceptical about such books, I decided to buy the e-book anyway.

While placing the order, I had an idea. The author described the events of the film very precisely, so it makes sense to look up stills from the film and refresh my memory of the main characters’ faces and names.
