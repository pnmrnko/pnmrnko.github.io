I watched the new third season of Twin Peaks. My impression: the first two seasons were better. They captured that warm small-town atmosphere so well: strong black coffee, cherry pie and a tangle of strange events that more or less unravelled by the end of the second season.

The third season has a lot of everything: characters, mysteries, strange surreal scenes, but something is still missing. The third season is interesting, but harder to take in. You need to rewatch it a few more times to understand at least something.

But I liked one thing. At the end of some episodes, a band plays at the Bang Bang Bar. Great ones: Sharon Van Etten, Au Revoir Simone and Chromatics.
