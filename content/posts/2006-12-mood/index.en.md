I was in a good mood this morning. Even though the weather was bad, I started the day laughing. I usually get up late, but today I got up right on schedule. I made myself some muesli, got dressed quickly, glanced at the clock — and there were still 30 minutes before school opened. A cartoon was on at that time, so to fill the time I turned on the TV.

Everything is fine at school, nothing interesting.

After school I came home after five lessons, because there was no English class. I walked home with Grandma, because I let slip that Mum wasn’t well. I had been ill for a whole month and she caught it from me. Grandma scolded Mum a little for getting sick and left. In the evening I thought it would be good to do at least one lesson for Monday, and I got so carried away that I accidentally left only one lesson for Sunday.

It makes sense to write my daily schedule down in this notebook:

*Weekdays* \
6:30 — wake up \
By 7:00 — eat \
By 7:15 — get dressed (15 min for cartoons) \
7:30 — leave for school \
8:00–14:30 — school \
15:00 — lunch \
15:30–16:30 — rest \
16:00–19:00 — homework \
19:00 — dinner

*Weekends* \
7:00 — get up \
7:30–8:30 — watch cartoons \
9:00 — breakfast \
9:30 — rest mixed with homework (14:00 snack) \
19:00 — dinner \
21:00 — bed
