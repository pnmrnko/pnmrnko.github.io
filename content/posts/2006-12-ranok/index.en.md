I didn’t get up very cheerful. As soon as I woke up, I turned on the TV. NTN was showing the cartoon “Jim and Jerry”, then I switched to M1 and watched until breakfast.

The morning started with arguments. My dad is so stubborn. I don’t like him. First (most importantly), he won’t let me watch TV. Second, Dad had just been watching TV and turned it off. I turned it on again to watch some shows. And he came in, took the remote and switched to the news.

I don’t understand him. Every day he asks us to give him a massage (makes us, rather than asks). And right now I’m sitting at the table writing, while he watches his “Channel 5”.

Mum has just called out: “Breakfast is ready!” \
“Go on then,” says Dad. And watches TV for another half an hour.
