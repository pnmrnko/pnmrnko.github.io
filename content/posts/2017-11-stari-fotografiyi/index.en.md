I came across a [website with photographs](<http://mentallandscape.com/C_Catalog.htm>) taken by Soviet spacecraft. Former scientist Don Mitchell has gathered loads of rare images in one place. In his words, the history of Soviet planetary and lunar probes has been neglected in the West, the photos from those missions are very hard to find, and his catalogue collects most of the available images of the Moon, Mars and Venus.

A panorama of the surface of Venus taken by the Venera 13 lander on March 1, 1984.

{{< photo src="C_Venera14_New2.jpg" >}}

I wonder what could be left of this probe after thirty-three years on a planet with a temperature of 470 °C?

And this is the Moon, photographed on October 24, 1970 by Zond 8.

{{< photo src="C_Zond08_X38detail.jpg" >}}
