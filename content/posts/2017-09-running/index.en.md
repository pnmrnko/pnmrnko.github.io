When my legs are moving, when my body is moving, I simply let my brain breathe. And I use this time as my own — time to think, to bring out memories, problems I need to deal with, stresses I might have. I let all of it go, and the anxiety disappears completely and stays behind me.

In this state, physical tiredness stops being a problem. You just find yourself in your own head, enjoying the sun on your face, the wind or the rain, or whatever it is, minding your own business alone with yourself.
