## The First Desk

The bell for break has just rung. Everyone ran off to the canteen. Ten minutes ago (10:40) I was reading a text aloud. It’s an assignment you get a mark for. I got 11 points out of 12. Our teacher is going to a seminar until the end of the first term. L. V. will stand in for her. The girls are trying to sort out the dances. For New Year they came up with a sketch and handed out the roles. They just can’t come up with a flight attendants’ dance.

I can’t write events down that fast, so I’m keeping it short.

## At Home

Lessons are over and I came home. I showed the presents Tanya L. gave me. I absolutely have to give her something for March 8. But March 8 is still a long way off. I can’t wait for New Year. It’s December 6 and there’s still no snow. Although there was frost this morning, the weather is perfect for me. This is the kind of weather I like most. Sunshine, not a cloud in the sky, no wind, and most importantly — a light frost.

## 15:05

Before doing my homework, I look into my school diary to check for hard subjects. This time there aren’t any, except maths.

## 17:40

Maths turned out not to be as easy as I thought. I barely solved two problems, and the last one — I struggled with it for two hours. A sneaky division sign was standing where a multiplication sign should have been. When I found the mistake, I nearly died of anger. I struggled for two hours over a problem, and it was just some pathetic division sign instead of a multiplication sign!
