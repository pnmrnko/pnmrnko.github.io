Although this short film is a few years old now, it still inspires me. These fantastic scenes are made incredibly realistic. And what about Carl Sagan’s epic words! Take a look:

{{< embed provider="youtube" id="YH3c1QZzRK4" title="Wanderers — a short film by Erik Wernquist" ratio="40/17" >}}

The narration is Carl Sagan reading from his book *Pale Blue Dot*: about how, after hundreds of generations of settled life, the road still quietly calls us, like a nearly forgotten song from childhood; about the restless minority driven toward unexplored lands and new worlds; and about the other worlds that, silently, in orbit around the Sun, are waiting.
