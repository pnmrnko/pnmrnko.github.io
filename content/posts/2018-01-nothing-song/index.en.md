For more than a year I’ve been listening to the American folk-pop duo *Lily & Madeleine*. Lily and Madeleine aren’t just friends, they’re sisters, who started recording covers and uploading them to YouTube back in high school. They now have three full albums. If you like American folk music, be sure to listen to their songs. Start with this song:

{{< embed provider="bandcamp" id="3216491533" title="Lily & Madeleine — Come To Me" caption="There’s a [music video](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q3Wm5pV30tc>) too: Madge on the left, Lily on the right" >}}

Their tracks are very melodic and sensitive, and their latest album, *Keep It Together*, turned out even better than the previous ones. The last track on it, *Nothing*, was written by Madeleine, and it’s one of her favourite songs. On their website she explains that the song dramatises a personal experience: while writing the album she thought about her romantic relationships and friendships and how those bonds affected her, for better or worse (or both at once), and the songs let her compare who she was a year ago with who she is becoming.
